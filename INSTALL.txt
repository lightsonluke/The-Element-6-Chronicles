WHAT CHANGED
1. Tall platform moved farther right and enlarged.
   - Artwork: x=1450, width=380, top reference y=105.
   - Solid invisible collider: x=1536, y=105, width=208, height=520.
   - It remains solid and is NOT drop-through.
2. Main platform underside collision no longer treats the entire underside
   width as a side wall. The ceiling collision catches upward contact from
   below; side-wall resolution is limited to the narrow left/right edge faces.
   This prevents touching the underside from teleporting/shoving the fighter
   sideways while retaining the main platform's side collisions.
3. The main platform's shortened underside depth and the three independent
   drop-through bridge sections are preserved.

The collision shapes are invisible. The clean Silver's Mansion platform art
continues to be rendered by the existing renderer assets; reference-guide PNGs
are not used as platform artwork.
