WHAT CHANGED

Silver's Mansion:
- The main floor keeps its existing clean artwork and now uses one invisible solid rectangular collider: the guided top edge, full-height left/right side walls, and a bottom/underside aligned with the actual bottom edge of the platform artwork.
- The ornamental bridge has three separate invisible one-way colliders. The left and right thick decks have their own higher collision lines; the thin center span has its own lower collision line.
- The tall stone platform is moved farther right: its artwork begins at the main floor artwork's right edge, and its solid collider is separated from the main floor collider. It is not drop-through and supports wall sliding. The camera follows fighters as play moves toward it.
- The supplied collision-guide images are not used as platform artwork. The existing clean Silver's Mansion artwork and renderer remain unchanged.

Wall-slide departure rewards:
- Only actual solid-wall slide contact arms a departure reward. One-way platforms cannot trigger it.
- Leaving the wall grants exactly two airborne jumps and one independent air dodge.
- Spending either jump no longer reduces the air-dodge inventory.
- The reward can be granted on three separate wall departures before landing. A fourth departure grants nothing.
- Touching the wall again re-arms the next departure. Landing resets the chain and restores normal resources.
- A dash away from a wall does not count as a wall-slide departure.
