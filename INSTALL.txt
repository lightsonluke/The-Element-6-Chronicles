SILVER'S MANSION COLLISION UPDATE — V4

Replacement files:
- PlatformFighter.jsx
- fighter.js

Install:
1. Back up your current files.
2. Replace PlatformFighter.jsx and fighter.js with the versions in this package.
3. Keep the files in the same directories as their existing counterparts.

Change in this version:
- The main platform's underside/bottom collision depth is reduced from 302px to 101px (approximately one-third of its previous size, removing about two-thirds).
- The top collision remains at y=570.
- The left and right side-wall collision still extends to the platform base at y=872. Only the underside collision depth is shortened; the side walls are not shortened.
- All collision remains invisible. The original platform artwork is unchanged.

The other Silver's Mansion platform positions and the previous wall-movement changes are preserved from V3.

Validation note: fighter.js passed Node's syntax check. This package has not been tested in a live browser/gameplay session.
