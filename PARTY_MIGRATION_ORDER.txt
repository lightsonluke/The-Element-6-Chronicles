After the clan tournament schema, activity tracking, and weekly reward
migrations, apply Supabase-clan-reward-member-scale.sql last. It replaces the
old fixed monthly pool with 7,000 Tokens per current member, shared according
to each member's recorded tournament-period XP.

The Table settings migration adds the last_seen_at field and selectable total
fighter count of 2–8. Apply it before the Honored Bot launch migration so that
the launch RPC can read match_settings. The start RPC fills the remaining
selected slots with Honored Bots and validates the connected human count.

Do not rerun migrations if your deployment process manages them separately.
